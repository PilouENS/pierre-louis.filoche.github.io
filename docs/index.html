Internship - International Laboratory on Learning Systems (ILLS), CNRS

May - July 2025

3 months internship
Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures increase model capacity at near-constant computational cost through sparse expert activation controlled by a router. This project investigates whether it is possible to predict, from the immediate textual context, which experts are activated by an MoE-based LLM across layers, with two objectives: (i) to anticipate expert loading (preloading), and (ii) to better understand routing dynamics. We propose several predictors: deterministic statistical models (context depth 1–5), a lightweight MLP, and an LSTM operating on token embeddings reduced via PCA (or learned dimensionality reduction). We analyze the trade-offs between accuracy and model size, the impact of context depth, and the role of embedding dimensionality reduction.

Supervised by Loris Marchal and Pablo Piantanida
